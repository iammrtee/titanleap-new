Questions founders ask

What's the difference between the free look and the $297 audit?

The free look is one problem, the biggest one, sent by Triumph within 24 hours. The 5-Hour Customer Leak Audit is the full picture: your three biggest leaks, the exact fixes, how many customers each could win back, your best channels for new customers, and a 90-day plan. Delivered within 5 hours, refunded if it finds nothing.

Is this just an AI report?

No. Software does the first pass over your pages. Then a person reads every finding, checks the numbers against your situation and removes anything generic. Nothing reaches you unread.

Do you just fix my site, or help me get new customers too?

Both. The audit shows where you lose customers today and names the best channels to find new ones. The monthly plans run that acquisition for you: content, funnels and outreach.

Do I need to get on a call?

No. Everything happens over email. If you want to talk it through afterwards, just reply.

What do you need from me?

Your website, what you sell and to whom, your rough traffic and conversion numbers if you have them, and what you think is holding growth back. It takes about two minutes.

I haven't launched yet. Is it still useful?

Yes. We review your positioning, landing page and signup flow before you pay for traffic. We'll also tell you where your first customers are most likely to come from.

Where do the dollar ranges come from?

Your traffic, conversion rates and prices, compared with targets that are realistic for your stage. We give ranges rather than single numbers, because anyone quoting an exact figure is guessing.

What happens after the audit?

Nothing, unless you want it to. Most founders make the first fix themselves. If you want us to build the fixes, the $297 comes off a Sprint or a monthly plan.
